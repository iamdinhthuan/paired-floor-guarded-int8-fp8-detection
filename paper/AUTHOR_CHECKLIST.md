# Author checklist before submission

The scientific source is internally validated, but the following statements require confirmation by the authors and must not be inferred automatically.

- [x] **Funding:** the authors confirmed that the research received no specific funding; copy the no-specific-grant statement from `main.tex` into the submission system.
- [x] **Authorship:** the author order, spelling, affiliations, corresponding-author email, ORCIDs, and CRediT roles were confirmed by the submitting author.
- [ ] **Generative-AI declaration:** verify that OpenAI Codex, OpenAI ChatGPT, and Anthropic Claude Code are the complete and accurate tool list, and that the described human review matches actual use.
- [x] **Graphical abstract:** the submitting author confirmed that the graphical abstract is author-created and that the authors hold the necessary rights; upload `graphical_abstract.png` as the separate Graphical Abstract item.
- [ ] **Data/code availability:** confirm that the uploaded files match the availability statement and that no restricted dataset, checkpoint, engine, prediction, credential, or personal data is included.
- [x] **Supplementary upload mapping:** the submitting author confirmed the mapping: `preview/supplement.pdf` as Supplementary Information S1 and `supplementary_data_s2.zip` as Supplementary Data S2.
- [x] **Public archive:** release `v2.0.0` is archived at DOI `10.5281/zenodo.22031664`; verify that the final submission metadata uses this exact version-specific DOI.
- [ ] **Submission metadata:** copy the final title, abstract, keywords, highlights, declarations, and corresponding-author details exactly into Editorial Manager.
- [ ] **Final visual check:** open the uploaded PDF generated by the journal system and inspect equations, author affiliations, figures, tables, and references after conversion.
