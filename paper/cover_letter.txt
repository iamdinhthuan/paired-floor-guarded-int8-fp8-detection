Dear Editors of Image and Vision Computing,

Please consider our manuscript, “A Paired, Floor-Guarded Evaluation Protocol for TensorRT INT8 and FP8 Object Detectors under Synthetic Image Corruptions,” for publication as a Full-Length Research Paper in Image and Vision Computing.

The manuscript addresses a recurring measurement problem in quantized computer vision: a raw accuracy difference on corrupted images combines the format discrepancy already present on matched-clean inputs with any additional corruption-specific interaction. We introduce a byte-matched, image-paired four-cell protocol that separates these components and requires the signed interaction to be interpreted alongside absolute corrupted accuracy. This design is evaluated across four datasets, three YOLO11 capacities, four synthetic corruption families, and three severities, with untouched VOC/KITTI final partitions and targeted sensitivity analyses for corruption realizations, training and calibration seeds, class-universe preservation, aggregation weights, and architecture transfer.

The methodological distinction changes the empirical interpretation. Although the raw corrupted gap favors FP8 in 134 of 144 exploratory cells, subtracting the matched-clean gap changes the interpretation in 56 cells. FP8 preserves 1.40 more matched-clean AP on average, while the balanced clean-adjusted interaction is near zero in the broader grid and negative on the untouched VOC/KITTI holdout. Absolute AP nevertheless decreases in 282 of 288 corrupted arms. Architecture stress cases further show that a favorable interaction sign can occur after a clean-fidelity failure, underscoring why clean fidelity, absolute corrupted accuracy, and corruption-specific interaction must be reported separately.

We believe the paper fits Image and Vision Computing because its primary contribution is a general, auditable performance-evaluation protocol for executable object-detection systems, supported by extensive quantitative comparisons and reproducibility checks. The manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and all authors have approved its submission. The research received no specific grant from funding agencies in the public, commercial, or not-for-profit sectors. The remaining disclosure and graphical-abstract confirmations will be completed in the journal submission system before final upload.
